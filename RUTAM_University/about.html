About RUTAM

Purpose, people and possibilities

Our story

A fictional university created for a web-development assignment

Ruthvik University of Technology and Management (RUTAM) is a fictional institution used in this project. The website demonstrates the structure and visual language commonly found on contemporary university websites.

Its academic model emphasizes technology, management, practical learning, research, student development and industry readiness.

Vision

Develop capable, ethical and innovative graduates prepared for a changing world.

Mission

Combine rigorous academics with practical experience, creativity and collaboration.

Values

Integrity, inclusion, curiosity, responsibility, excellence and continuous learning.
