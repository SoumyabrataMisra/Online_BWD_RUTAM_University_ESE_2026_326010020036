# Ruthvik University of Technology and Management (RUTAM)

A complete multi-page university website project using HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript. 
The information architecture is inspired by the way modern Indian university websites organize 
Academics, Admissions, Campus Life, About, Gallery and Contact content. It is an original fictional 
project and is not an official Marwadi University website.

## Pages
- index.html - Home
- programs.html - Academics / Programmes
- admissions.html - Admissions enquiry
- campus.html - Facilities and student life
- about.html - University overview
- gallery.html - Real-photo gallery
- contact.html - Contact form with JavaScript validation

## Technologies
HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5.3, JavaScript.

## Run locally
1. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
2. Internet access is recommended because Bootstrap and the photography are loaded from online sources.

## Important
- RUTAM is fictional and the contact details are sample details.
- Replace sample contact information before real-world use.
- The forms are front-end demos and do not send email without a server/backend.
- The photos are real Unsplash images. See images/README.txt for sources and replace them with your own 
licensed institutional photos when deploying commercially.
